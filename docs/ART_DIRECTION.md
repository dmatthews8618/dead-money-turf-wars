# Art direction — GTA 1997 × Dope Wars

## Visual identity
Original top-down crime-game atmosphere, inspired by late-1990s PC games without copying their artwork or assets. Wet asphalt, sodium streetlights, battered cars, industrial estates, railway arches, brick terraces, graffiti, police cordons and neon signs. Grimy, readable, and not excessively dark.

## Interface
- Keep the compact market/trading screen as the primary play surface.
- Add a 2D district map with six neighbourhoods and clear markers for each competing agent.
- Show gang influence, police pressure and contested turf at a glance.
- Maintain mobile-friendly touch targets and readable prices.
- Use restrained pixel-art or low-resolution sprites; avoid AI-looking decorative overload.

## Gameplay expansion order
1. District map with player positions and travel events.
2. Gang territory pressure affecting trade and travel.
3. Police heat with escalating consequences and ways to lie low.
4. Rival interference and temporary cooperation against common threats.
5. Fictional weapons and vehicles as abstract game upgrades with costs and tradeoffs.
6. Event replay and per-agent memory summaries.

## Design constraint
The game is a strategy simulation, not a real-time action game. Every added mechanic should produce meaningful decisions that the two AI competitors can explain and remember.

## Model honesty
Until actual APIs are connected, GPT and Claude remain labelled simulated strategy bots.
